The OBR's inputs

What the levers imply for each of the OBR's economic inputs, year by year. You can override any cell (amber); the levers then leave it alone. Overriding breaks the consistency the levers provide, so check the scenario still hangs together. Red values are large enough that the OBR's linear elasticities may not hold. The box in each cell is the change from the OBR's forecast; the grey line under it is the OBR's own forecast for that year, for reference (earnings and house prices as growth on a year earlier; employment and unemployment as 16+ rates; durables as a share of consumer spending and imports as a share of GDP). The two answer different questions, so they needn't move together.
