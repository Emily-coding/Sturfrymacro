Tax ready reckoner

What raising or cutting the main UK taxes would raise or cost, built on HMRC's direct effects of illustrative tax changes. Set any number of changes and the page adds up HMRC's estimates. Figures are changes in receipts compared with current policy, including how people respond to the change, but not the wider effects on the economy.

Made by AI in conjunction with economists. Figures may contain errors: not official statistics or financial advice.
