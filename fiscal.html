Public finances ready reckoner

How changes in the economy feed through to tax, spending, borrowing and debt. Built on the OBR's ready reckoner: set the levers, and the page translates them into the OBR's economic inputs and applies its elasticities. Results are changes from the OBR's forecast, not forecasts in themselves.

Which taxes and spending lines move

Change by line, £ billion (positive = more revenue or more spending). Lines that don't move are left out.

What drives it

Change in borrowing from each OBR input on its own, £ billion. These don't add up exactly to the total: the triple lock, the rounding used for uprating and the GDP denominators aren't linear.

The OBR's inputs

What the levers imply for each of the OBR's economic inputs, year by year. You can override any cell (amber); the levers then leave it alone. Overriding breaks the consistency the levers provide, so check the scenario still hangs together. Red values are large enough that the OBR's linear elasticities may not hold.
